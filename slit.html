Young's double slit experiment

Superposition: where the two circular waves cross, their heights simply add together — that's all superposition means, waves stacking on top of each other.

Interference: the visible result of that adding. Where two crests line up, they add to a bigger crest (constructive interference → bright fringe). Where a crest meets a trough, they cancel out (destructive interference → dark fringe).

Shorter wavelength or wider slit spacing → fringes squeeze closer together. Longer wavelength or narrower spacing → fringes spread further apart. Fringe spacing ∝ λ / d.
